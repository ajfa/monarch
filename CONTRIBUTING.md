# Contributing

Contributions are welcome.

(see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)).

Contributions require agreeing to the **Contributor License Agreement** in
[`CLA.md`](CLA.md). You keep the copyright in your work; the agreement lets the
project keep shipping under the GPL while leaving the owner able to relicense
the project as a whole later. Signing is one line in your pull request
description — the CLA explains how.

Read [`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md) first: parts of this repository are other
people's work and are **not** covered by the project license. Do not submit
changes that would relicense them, and do not bring in material of unclear
origin.

## Practical

1. **Build and run whatever checks the repository has before and after.**
2. **Say how you verified a behavioural claim.** "It looks right" is not a
   check; a test that fails before your change and passes after it is.
3. **Small commits with a real message.** Say what changed and *why*.
4. **Sign off your commits** with `git commit -s`.
5. **English only** for prose, comments, identifiers and commit messages.
   Hardware labels stay in the language they are printed in on the machine.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue with: what you did, what you expected, what happened, and enough
detail to reproduce it. If it is a fidelity question about real hardware, cite
the documentation you are going by.
